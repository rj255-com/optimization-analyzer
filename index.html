    How one answer is built

    1. ParseOnly x, y, numbers and approved functions are accepted.
    2. DifferentiateSymPy gives the derivatives exactly.
    3. SolveExact roots, or Brent’s method and multi-start solving when no closed form exists.
    4. ClassifySecond derivative test, first derivative test, or the Hessian.
    5. VerifySciPy searches on its own and must agree.
